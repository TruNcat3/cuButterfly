# A100 closeout — 2026-11-07

The defined, frozen A100 work is complete. This closes a bounded experiment
list, not every Cartesian combination, every external baseline, or the
long-term cross-platform goal. Search and calibration are engineering means;
this closeout adds no ranking competition or new acceptance threshold.

The subsequently authorized [November 8 representative profiles](REPRESENTATIVE_PROFILES_20261108.md)
are also complete: two requests, twelve variants, three-process timings and
matched complete-call captures. This bounded supplement adds mechanism
attribution on a separate A100 80GB device without changing the frozen matrix.
