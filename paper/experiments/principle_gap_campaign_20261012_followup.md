# Remaining Evidence Inventory

This planning-only version extends
[the frozen initial campaign](principle_gap_campaign_20261012.json) without changing
its launch specification, source identity or four executable layout cases.
The [follow-up JSON](principle_gap_campaign_20261012_followup.json) records priorities,
dependencies, existing paths and completion conditions. It creates no additional
GPU queue and grants no application restart or implicit matrix expansion.

| Priority | Remaining Evidence | Status and Completion Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | E1 original36 independent repetition | Two additional processes per original row/backend; replay compatible accepted mappings, retain gaps and report three-process distributions. NTT extension coverage is separate. No new rows are added. |
| 1 | QuaRot B1/B4/B8 | Reference the existing GPU1 queue and collect its accepted releases. Do not restart tuning or another model. Require two processes, exchanged order, identical model/input, cache replay and bitwise validation. |
| 2 | Missing external operator columns | Zeta, structured, word32 NTT and FP64 FWHT need real matched GPU implementations/adapters or documented absence. Do not substitute another precision, modulus or operation. |
| 2 | NCU achieved occupancy and L2 hit rate | Registers/shared allocation and L2 sector bytes are insufficient. Query target-supported metrics and capture named accepted pairs under a GPU2-bound diagnostic driver; retain per-kernel denominators and weights. No profile matrix is implicitly scheduled. |
| 2 | Online search and setup costs | Observe actual public enumeration/ranking/search/compilation/setup/first-use under isolated cold/hit policies and fixed budgets. Offline16-pool evidence does not substitute for online cost. |
| 3 | CKKS complete RNS application | Connect all RNS limbs, moduli and ciphertext dependencies to a complete CKKS application, native HEonGPU competitor and application oracle. A single-limb adapter probe does not complete this task. |

Component-disabled re-search and portable second-leaf/core transfer remain the
implementation dependencies in the initial JSON. Existing historical FFT leaf
factorial and E3 fixed-physical-group/local-partition controls are reused with
their original identities. No task in this inventory changes the finite new
layout study or mixes its kernel timing with complete application timing.

The first local run also exposed an error-report interface dependency for the
fixed-mapping FWHT benchmark. Its existing sanity check does not provide the
full error distribution requested by the user. The exact completion contract
is recorded as `missing_dependency` in
`../evidence/PRINCIPLE_GAP_RUN_20261012.json`; the measured timings remain
exploratory until that reporting gap is filled and numerical acceptance is
addressed separately.
