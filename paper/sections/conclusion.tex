\section{Conclusion}
\system{} derives a shared execution construction from butterfly dependencies.
Dependency-closed subgraphs identify local work, hierarchical hybrid dataflow
combines residency and streaming, and successor-aligned writeback connects
their representations. Arithmetic, workload, and hardware inputs specialize
this construction through compatible cores and bounded search. Measured
fixed-core controls support an organization benefit across the tested
arithmetic families. Library comparisons are competitive on the reported
cohorts, while application integration and broader semantic coverage remain
distinct requirements for establishing general benefit.
